01 Interactive Portfolio Room +
3D ENVIRONMENT / INTERACTIVE DESIGN

Interactive Portfolio Room

A stylized, interactive 3D environment designed and modeled in Blender. Inspired by vintage interiors, the room features animated vinyl records, a record player, custom lighting, and a miniature character. I brought the environment into Three.js to create an interactive experience that visitors can explore.

  • Blender
  • Three.js
  • 3D Modeling
  • Lighting
  • Animation
Explore the Room
✳
Want to explore this room?

Behind the Environment

I designed the room as a way to combine my interests in 3D art and interactive web development. Each element was created to contribute to the atmosphere of the scene, from the stylized furniture and decorations to the lighting and character animations.

One of my biggest goals was making the environment feel alive while keeping it optimized for a website. I used Blender animations, GLB exports, and Three.js to bring the scene into the browser.
